## What can be written now

1. **Methods.** Draft the historical phase and the scenario phase: 2023 CRT calibration, AR5 aggregation, three scenario definitions, linear interpolation, included processes and exclusions.
2. **Results 1: historical trend.** Use the prepared figures and tables: 1990–1996 decline, recovery to 2023, category composition and CRF–CRT comparison.
3. **Results 2: activity-data comparability.** Present clinker, lime, pig iron, steel, ferroalloys, aluminium and zinc as cases with distinct comparability status.
4. **Results 3 and Discussion.** Report 2030/2050 results as exploratory and discuss why results depend on the flat baseline, selected boundary and emissions multipliers rather than being a technology forecast.

The bilingual [scenario protocol](leap_scenarios_2023_2050_en.md) supplies the exact results table, figure captions and article-safe wording.

## What still cannot be claimed

- results for the entire national IPPU sector;
- technology-specific adoption, costs, investment needs or an optimal policy sequence;
- compliance with national targets; or
- empirical feasibility of the assumed reductions.

The current pathways are not evidence for these claims; they are transparent sensitivity trajectories. After the desktop-LEAP verification, they may be called LEAP-calculated only within this same boundary.
